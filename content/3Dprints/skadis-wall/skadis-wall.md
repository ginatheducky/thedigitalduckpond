# Less searching. More making.
Four organizers for my IKEA SKADIS setup, brought together in one collection. Pick the pieces that work for your space and print your own.

In this collection:
- Scissors
- Pliers
- Pens
- Thread

# Meet the prints.
One wall. Four little solutions.

Scissor holder: A dedicated space for your scissors, ready for the next cut.
Plier holder: Give your pliers a home in your tool setup.
Pen organizer: An organizing insert for your SKADIS container, with a place for pens and pencils.
Overlock thread holder: A place for your overlock thread in your sewing corner.

# Build your own little corner.
Start with the thing you're always looking for. Add another organizer when you need it. 

Printing notes: Each design has its own files and instructions. Follow the linked listing for the piece you want to print.
